# Case Study and Portfolio Playbook (applies to every build)

Use this when the `template-showcase` agent or the owner packages any build for Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn or a website. Source: the n8n twin agent's guidance, adopted as the shared standard for both platforms.

## What makes a case study convert
- **One reader, one question.** The buyer asks "can this person fix my problem?" They want to see their own situation, not your tools.
- **Open with the outcome, not the tech.** "Leads reach the right person in about a minute" beats "built a Make scenario with an LLM."
- **Order:** problem, approach, result, what's next. One scroll.
- **Two lengths:** a short version of about 150 words (Upwork, LinkedIn) and a long version of about 500 words. The long one does not repeat the short one.
- **Every number has a source.** Say what was measured, how, and over what period. A small honest number beats a big vague one. Mark estimates as estimates.
- **Before and after in the buyer's terms:** hours, errors, wait time, cost. Never node or module counts.
- **Show proof:** screenshots, the Flow Studio recording, a test report excerpt, and a named quote if one exists.
- **Show how you work:** one line each on the approval gates, the testing and the handover. This separates you from a cheap gig.
- **End with one small next step.** "Send me your form and CRM."

## Never
- Call a demo a client result. Label it "Demo project, synthetic data" until a real client exists.
- Invent testimonials, metrics or logos. One fake claim costs every real one.
- List every module.
- Use a client's name, logo, quote or data without written permission. Anonymize by default.

## Portfolio kit rules
- **One kit, cut down per platform.** Build the full kit once, then trim.
- **Lead with the strongest asset:** the Flow Studio recording. It shows the system working and is rarer than a screenshot.
- **Match the offer to the platform.** Upwork buyers compare proposals: offer a fixed scope with a clear first step. Fiverr buyers compare packages: three tiers with a clear difference.
- **Every claim is checkable.** Each number links to how it was measured.
- **Review quarterly.** Drop anything that no longer matches how you work.
- **Check the platform's own rules before exporting** (image sizes, video length, listing fields, link and contact rules). They change, and nothing here states current limits.
