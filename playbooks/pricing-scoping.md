# Scoping & Pricing Guide (for freelance gigs)

Use during intake to size a gig and draft a quote. These are starting heuristics for the owner to adjust; they are not market data.

## Complexity tiers
| Tier | Typical shape | Build effort guide |
|---|---|---|
| Simple | 1 trigger, 2–4 modules, 1–2 apps, no AI | few hours |
| Standard | 5–12 modules, router/filter, 2–4 apps, error handling + alerts | 1–2 days |
| Advanced | AI step with guardrails, multiple scenarios, data store, review queue | 2–5 days |
| Complex | Multi-system sync, high volume, custom API calls, approvals | 1+ week |

## Add-ons (price separately)
Loom walkthrough, extended testing, monitoring retainer, extra integrations, revisions beyond the agreed rounds, training session.

## Make cost check
Operations/month = ops per run × runs per month. Compare to the client's Make plan (client pays their own Make unless the owner decides to resell under their plan). Always state this in the quote.

## Quote must state
Scope in/out, number of revision rounds, what the client provides (access, sample data, approvals), timeline, test-evidence deliverable, support window, and that the automation is delivered inactive until sign-off.
