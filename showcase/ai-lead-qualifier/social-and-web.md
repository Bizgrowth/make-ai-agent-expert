## LinkedIn post (about 150 words)
Most small businesses lose good leads in the first hour.

Not because the leads were bad. Because nobody looked at them in time.

I built a Make.com workflow that fixes that:

1. A new lead fills in the form
2. AI scores how well they fit, using a rubric written in plain English
3. The lead lands in the CRM with the score and the reason
4. Hot leads trigger an instant Slack alert and a follow-up task
5. Anything the AI is unsure about goes to a person, not into the pipeline

The part I care most about is step 5. Automation should tell you who to call first, and it should hand you the hard calls.

## Presentation script (maps to the Flow Studio SOP phases)
1. **Trigger fires.** "Every automation starts with one event. Here, a form submission." Point at the TRIGGER bubble.
2. **Bundle created.** "Make packages the data into a bundle that travels along the line." Point at the moving card.
3. **Modules run.** "Each app does one job: clean, check duplicates, score with Claude, save to HubSpot." Point at the +1 op markers.
4. **Filter and router.** "A router splits the flow by result: hot, needs review, warm or cold."
5. **Error handling.** "If it's a duplicate or the AI is unsure, the run stops or asks a person. This is what makes it safe to sell."
6. **Response.** "The caller gets an answer, so the form knows it worked."
7. **Execution log.** "Every run is recorded with status, time and credits. That is your proof."
Close with: "Tell me your lead volume and I will estimate the monthly Make cost before we start."
