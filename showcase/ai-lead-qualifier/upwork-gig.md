## Short pitch (first lines of the description)
Stop letting good leads wait. I build a Make.com automation that reads each new lead, scores how well they fit your business, saves them to your CRM, and alerts you the moment a hot one arrives. Anything the AI is unsure about goes to a person, not into your pipeline.

## What you get
- A Make.com scenario connecting your form, an AI model, HubSpot (or your CRM) and Slack or email
- A scoring rubric tuned to your business, written in plain English and easy to change
- Hot, warm and cold routing, with alerts only for the leads that matter
- Duplicate protection and a human-review path for uncertain cases
- A test report showing each scenario was tested before go-live
- A plain-English process document: what it does, what to watch, how to pause it
- Optional: a short recorded walkthrough
- Delivered switched off. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

## How it works
Form submitted → cleaned and checked → duplicate check → AI scores the lead → result verified → saved to your CRM → hot leads create a task and alert you, unsure ones ask a person, the rest are logged quietly.
